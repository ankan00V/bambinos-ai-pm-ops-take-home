# SOP for teachers

This is what a teacher does after a parent pays for a renewal. It is shorter than the current process on purpose.

## What changed for you

You no longer fill a renewal form for a child who is staying in your batch, on your days, at your time. The payment already tells us the student, the class, the batch, the teacher and the amount. We schedule from that.

You only hear from us when something needs a decision that only you can make.

## The five things you may be asked

**1. A payment that does not match a student.** We will send the invoice number and ask you to confirm the child. Reply with the name, nothing else.

**2. A batch that is full.** We will ask one question: is this batch continuing next term? Reply yes or no. If yes, the child keeps the seat. If no, we find another batch and you do not need to do anything.

**3. A batch change the parent asked for.** We will tell you the new days and time. Reply only if the change breaks something on your side, such as a clash with another batch.

**4. A duplicate form.** If you submitted twice, we keep the first and close the second. You will get a one line message saying so. No action.

**5. A parent who wants a different teacher.** Ops handles the conversation. You will be told before the child moves, not after.

## Rules that save you time

- Do not send screenshots of payments. Send the invoice number. A screenshot cannot be checked by a machine and it slows the queue.
- Do not fill a form for a child who is not continuing. A form without a payment creates work for Ops and a false hope for the parent.
- If a parent says they paid but you cannot see it, tell Ops the child's name and the amount. Do not ask the parent to pay again.
- If a parent asks for a discount, do not promise one. Say Ops will call them. Discounts are recorded on the invoice, and an undocumented discount turns into an exception that holds the child's class.

## What happens after you reply

| Your action | What Ops does | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No form needed | Schedules the child within minutes | Nothing, unless there is a problem |
| Confirms a batch is continuing | Renews in place | A one line confirmation |
| Confirms a batch is closing | Moves the child to another batch | The new batch, days and time |
| Reports a mismatch | Checks the payment and calls the parent | A resolution message the same day |

## Response times we ask for

Under 4 hours for a payment or batch question. Under 24 hours for anything else. If you cannot reply in that window, reply "later today" and we will hold the case rather than escalate it.

## What we will never ask you to do

- Chase a parent for money. That is Ops and Finance.
- Decide a refund.
- Move a child into a batch you do not teach without being told.
- Re-type information that is already on the payment.
